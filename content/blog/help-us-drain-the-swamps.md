Years of "move fast and break things" left mature software companies buried in
software rot. I've come to think of it as the software swamps.

Coding agents don't drain those swamps. Left unchecked,
they dig them deeper in months instead of years — piling up thousands of brittle
tests in archaic frameworks, tangling dependencies, and turning a simple test
run into a ten-minute wait.

Asimi exists to reverse that. We're turning messy, slow codebases into
reliable, fast **software factories** — integrating with existing dev services
and running workflows that weave prompts and guardrails.

Demand from our design partners is picking up, and there is more swamp than one
person can drain alone. **Asimi is looking for a freelance engineer to help us
drain the swamps of software rot and build software factories.**

## Who you are

- **You've lived in mature codebases.** You recognize software rot on sight,
  understand how it happened without being smug about it, and genuinely enjoy
  cleaning it up.
- **You spend time learning new tools.** You care deeply about your env and infra.
- **You already work with coding agents.** You've seen both their magic and
  their follies, and you have strong opinions on how to harness them safely.
- **You're at home in the terminal.** Bonus points if `ZZ` is burned into your
  muscle memory.

## How we work

- **Mostly remote.** The rest of the time in Tel Aviv.
- **Real stakes, open code.** You'll work directly with me (Benny), dogfooding
  Asimi every day on both partner projects and the open-source tool itself.
- **Flexible freelance setup.** Looking for roughly half your time. We care
  about drained swamps and shipped work, not warming a chair on a fixed
  schedule.
